Privacy Policy

Last updated : November 13, 2026

In short: Emoty has no account and no servers that store your journal. What you write stays on your iPhone and is never used for statistics. There are no ads in the app. Outside your device, we only handle subscription data (through Apple and RevenueCat) anonymous usage statistics and the measurement of our TikTok ads, both of which you can turn off.

Who we are

Emoty is published by Ideonot (“we”). For any question about your data, contact us at support@ideonot.com.

Your journal

Your feelings, the emotions you choose, your personal affirmations, favorites and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s protected storage. We do not receive them and cannot read them. There is no cloud sync.

If you delete the app without a backup, this data is lost, because no copy exists anywhere else.

Emotion suggestions (Apple Intelligence)

On compatible iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Emoty suggests an emotion, an excerpt and spelling fixes using Apple’s on-device model. Your text is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to any third-party AI service. You can turn this off in the app’s settings. On other iPhones, you choose the emotion yourself.

Dictation

If you use dictation, Emoty asks for access to the microphone and speech recognition. Your voice is transcribed on your iPhone (on-device recognition) and the audio is not recorded or sent to us.

Backups

You can export an encrypted backup of your journal. It is protected by a passphrase that you choose and that we never know: we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Where you save or share the file (Files, iCloud Drive, email…) is your choice, and that service applies its own rules.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They never include what you wrote. Widgets display affirmations from the app and are generated on your device.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To manage subscriptions, we use RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase information (product, date, status, price, currency, country/storefront) and basic technical data (app version, iOS version, device model) needed to confirm your access to Premium. RevenueCat does not receive your journal. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

This data is kept as long as needed to manage your subscription and to meet accounting obligations.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how Emoty is used and improve it (for example, which onboarding step people leave, or whether they come back), the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, hosted in the European Union. These events say what happened, such as “feeling saved”, “paywall viewed” or “subscription started”, with a random identifier generated by the app, the app language, the app version, the device model, the iOS version and an approximate country. They never contain the content of your journal, the emotion you chose, your name or your email address, and they are not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

You can turn this off at any time in the app: Profile → “Anonymous statistics”. See PostHog’s privacy policy.

Measuring our TikTok ads

We advertise Emoty on TikTok. To know whether these ads work, the app uses TikTok’s official measurement kit (TikTok App Events SDK, TikTok Pte. Ltd. and affiliates). It tells TikTok only that the app was installed or opened, that the onboarding was completed, that the Premium offer was shown, and when a subscription starts (plan, price and currency), together with technical data such as the app version, iOS version and device model. It never receives your journal, your emotions, your name or your email address. The app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA): attribution relies on Apple’s privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork.

The same setting turns this off: Profile → “Anonymous statistics”. See TikTok’s privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, no email address or name collected by the app.
  • No ads in the app. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other companies’ apps and websites; the app would always ask your permission first.
  • No statistics on the content of your journal: usage statistics are anonymous and can be turned off (see above).
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Delete your data

Your journal and settings exist only on your iPhone: deleting the Emoty app permanently removes them. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected: delete them yourself if needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Since your journal never leaves your device, you control it directly. For subscription data, write to support@ideonot.com and include, if possible, the support identifier shown on the Premium screen of the app (for subscribers). You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

Emoty is not intended for children under 13 and some optional content contains mild language.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.

This website

This website currently uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security reasons.
